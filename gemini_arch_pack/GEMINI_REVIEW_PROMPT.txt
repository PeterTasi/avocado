   - Why this is high impact
   - What files/modules to change first
   - Migration strategy that minimizes regressions

- Explicitly distinguish correctness risk vs scalability risk.
- Call out assumptions and unknowns.
- Avoid introducing breaking API changes unless strongly justified.

Output format:
- Section A: Current Architecture Map
- Section B: Data Flow and Boundaries
- Section C: Risks with Evidence
- Section D: Refactor Roadmap (phased)
